# PhotoFrame map samples

- Val di Funes samples a 12 km-wide area centred at 46.6440° N, 11.7220° E from Mapzen Terrarium tiles. The saved grid contains 97 × 65 elevations rounded to metres. European terrain uses Copernicus data funded by the European Union, including the EU-DEM layers. [Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), [source credits](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md)
- Saint Petersburg extracts street and water geometry for a 6 km-wide area centred at 59.9410° N, 30.3000° E. Geometry is filtered to the overlay layers and projected into the local map view. The Base64 geometry fields hold little-endian 16-bit coordinates with a length prefix for each path.
